# Sheet panels: defenses, senses, saves, companions, mounts

How this system works today: the derived panels on the character sheet and the entities a character brings along.

> Moved verbatim out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-14 so it loads when needed instead of every turn. **This file is now the home for this material — update it here.** The standing rules that apply across systems stay in CLAUDE.md under "Character sheet rules"; what is still missing is in [docs/backlog.md](../backlog.md).

- **Summoned companions — mechanism built, 1 entry authored** (`companions/companionData.js` + the shared `CompanionPanel`). A companion is an entity a character conjures that has its own defensive numbers but no sheet of its own; the table is class-agnostic (`charClass`/`subclass`/`edition`/`minLevel`) and each entry supplies a `count(level)`, `stats(level)` and `traits(level)`. A stat carries either a plain string or a `buildBreakdown`, so a derived number is a clickable `BreakdownValue` like every other one on the sheet (`companion-{key}-stat-{stat}` → `…-breakdown`); traits collapse to their name via the shared `FeatureNote`. Rendered in the ClassSheet's **subclass features** area — data-driven, NOT registered in `config.subclassPanels`, so it needs no config entry and any config-driven class gets it free (a hand-written sheet does not; that's the same progressive gap as `speed-feat-note`). Authored: the **Echo Knight's echo** (AC 14 + PB via the exported `echoArmorClass`, which the Action Economy Manifest Echo card also reads so the two can't drift; 1 HP, conditions immunity, 1-minute duration, 30-ft leash, and two echoes at L18 for Legion of One). **Deliberately stateless** — no current HP, no "is it out right now", no position: the app displays what a character can do rather than simulating a battlefield (the Encounters-V1 line), and a 1-HP summon has nothing to track. A companion with real hit points (a familiar, a Steel Defender, a Drakewarden's drake, a mount) wants the Bestiary tie-in, not a bigger version of this table — see the mounts bullet below.

- **Mounts & vehicles — NOT MODELLED AT ALL.** Nothing tracks whether a character is mounted, what they're riding, or the mount's own AC/HP/speed/saves. Every mounted rule in the app is therefore prose: none of it can be computed, level-gated by the mount, or rolled. **When mounts/vehicles are built, revisit the mounted features already shipped as prose and decide which become real mechanics** — Cavalier **Born to the Saddle** (advantage on saves to avoid falling off; mount/dismount costs 5 ft instead of half your speed — the speed half could feed the movement model), Cavalier **Unwavering Mark**/**Ferocious Charger**/**Hold the Line** (all written assuming a mounted charge), the **Mounted Combatant** feat (advantage vs. smaller unmounted creatures — needs a target model; redirect-attack-to-self — needs a mount entity; **the mount's** Dexterity-save evasion — belongs on the MOUNT's sheet, which is why it is deliberately NOT in the Features Affecting Saves panel, whose scope is the character's own saves), and the **Mounted Combatant 2024** variant. Also unbuilt: mount/vehicle statblocks, the Bestiary tie-in (a mount is a creature), and speed/carrying rules. A mount is the same shape as a summoned companion (above) but with real hit points, so whatever models it should subsume `companionData`'s one stateless entry rather than sit beside it. Until then the honest surface for all of it is a prose rider or a feat card.

- **Senses (vision type + range) — BUILT** (`senses/senses.js` + `SensesPanel`, a **Senses** card in the CharacterDetail **Stats -> HP & Movement** sub-tab). Answers "how far can I see in the dark?", which the sheet could not: darkvision was granted in three places and displayed in NONE — the racial `Darkvision` trait, the Drow's `Superior Darkvision`, and the Rune Knight's Stone Rune all carried it as prose inside a description, so a player had to read three blurbs and work out which range won (found in QA on the Stone Rune). Wired CENTRALLY in CharacterDetail like the Defenses card, so hand-written sheets get it too, and gated on `hasSenses(ctx)` so ordinary vision costs no card. **Ranges SUPERSEDE, they do not stack** — a Drow Rune Knight sees 120 ft, not 180 — so `getSenses` keeps the largest per sense and returns the losers as `superseded` rather than dropping them, because a player who stows the rune-bearing axe needs to know they fall back to 60 ft rather than to nothing. Sources are the same `resolve(ctx)`-per-entry shape as `skillAdvantage.js` (race traits gate declaratively; the rune gates on `activeRunes()`, so unequipping removes the 120 ft). Sight-affecting traits that grant no radius (**Sunlight Sensitivity**) come back from `getSightNotes` and render name-only on the shared `FeatureNote` with their text read out of `RACE_TRAIT_DESCRIPTIONS`. Adding a source is a data row: the Stone Rune's is `passive.vision` in `runesData`, a race's is a `RACE_SENSE_TRAITS` entry. Deliberately NOT in scope: light sources (a torch is equipment), the blinded condition (a Conditions panel is the follow-up), blindsight/truesight/tremorsense (nothing in the app grants them yet — they are one row each when something does), and what a creature can see of YOU.

- **Damage defenses (resistances / immunities / flat reductions) — BUILT** (`defenses/defenses.js` + `DefensesPanel`, rendered as a **Defenses** card in the CharacterDetail **Stats → HP & Movement** sub-tab). Answers "is this fire damage halved?", which the sheet previously could not — every resistance in the app was prose inside a feature description. Wired CENTRALLY in CharacterDetail rather than through a sheet slot, so it works for data-driven **and** hand-written sheets alike (unlike the `afterHpNode` racial tracker); the card is gated on `hasDefenses(ctx)` so a character with none gets no empty card. **The rule the whole module is shaped around: the app has NO active-effect model** (no `isRaging`, no concentration, no durations — deliberate; same line companions/Encounters-V1/spell-granted-magic-weapons draw), so every entry is either `condition: null` (**Always on**) or carries the condition as visible text (**Situational**), and the two groups are never flattened. A bare "Bludgeoning" row for a Barbarian would be false most of the time. Rows are `{typeLabel, valueLabel, qualifier, condition}` — a `qualifier` narrows *which* damage of that type is covered ("from spells", "from nonmagical weapons", "except force"), because several RAW defenses key on the damage's SOURCE not its type; a `typeLabel` override names an unpersisted player choice ("Your choice" for the Fiend's rest-chosen type, "By patron" for the Genie) instead of inventing a type or dropping the row. Damage types may be a **function** of the character for stored choices (Dragonborn `draconic_ancestry.damage`, Sorcerer `draconic_bloodline.damage`). Feature rules text is read back out of `SUBCLASS_DATA`/`CLASS_FEATURES_*`/`RACE_TRAIT_DESCRIPTIONS`, so it can't drift; the name expands via the shared `FeatureNote` (NOT a copy of `SaveFeaturesPanel`, which hand-rolls its own collapse — that one is a missed reuse worth retro-fitting). **37 entries authored** across race/class/subclass. **FEATS come through the structured `damage_reduction` feat effect instead** (`featEffects.getFeatDamageReductions`, backend `seed_feats.py` `_damage_reduction()`; `amount` accepts the `'pb'` sentinel — 2024 Heavy Armor Master scales with PB and drops 2014's "nonmagical" clause), so a feat keeps ONE mechanization route. Deliberately EXCLUDED, each for a stated reason: reaction damage reduction (Deflect Missiles, Slow Fall, Spirit Shield, Song of Defense, Warding Maneuver → belongs on Action Economy), features that let you OVERCOME resistance (Ki-Empowered Strikes, Magic Arrow → `weaponMagic.js`), defenses granted only to OTHERS (Ancestral Protectors, Expansive Bond, Smite of Protection), condition-only immunity (Beguiling Defenses, Fungal Body, Divine Health — a different axis from damage; a **Conditions** panel is the follow-up), a summoned companion's own defenses (the Echo Knight echo's condition immunity → companion panel), and choices the app never persists (Totem Spirit's Bear, Elemental Epitome, Transmuter's Stone). **Feat `effects` are SNAPSHOTTED onto `character_data.feats[i]` at acquisition** (`FeatsSubTab`); re-running `seed_feats.py` now refreshes those snapshots (`sync_character_feat_snapshots`), so a new `/feat-effects` pass reaches existing characters after a re-seed.

- Features affecting the character's OWN saving throws — **mechanism built, 3 entries authored**. `skills/saveFeatures.js` + `SaveFeaturesPanel` list them under the Saving Throws grid (name only, click for the rules text), pulling descriptions out of the feature tables so nothing is retyped. Authored: Cavalier **Born to the Saddle**, Rune Knight **Hill Rune** (vs. poison, `applies`-gated on the rune being carved + equipped) and Rune Knight **Giant's Might**. An entry may now carry **`advantageAbilities: ['strength']`**, which puts an `adv` tag ON the save row itself (`save-advantage-{ability}`, teal, mirroring the skill grid's) plus an "Advantage — {feature}" line in that save's breakdown — `saveAdvantageSourcesFor(ability, ctx)` / `getSaveAdvantageAbilities(ctx)`. It is for advantage scoped by ABILITY only: advantage scoped by SITUATION (Born to the Saddle — falling off a mount; Hill Rune — against poison) deliberately omits the field and stays panel-only, since a tag on a save row asserts "roll this twice" with no room for the condition that makes it true. Giant's Might is also the first entry gated on an **active effect** rather than on what the character permanently has (`applies: isEffectActive(…, 'giants_might')`), so the tag appears exactly while the effect is switched on. Every further feature is a one-line data entry; the un-authored candidates are the ones the grid can't express as a number — Paladin Aura of Protection (+CHA), Fighter Indomitable (reroll), Monk Diamond Soul / 2024 Disciplined Survivor, Rogue Slippery Mind, Ranger Iron Mind, Barbarian Rage (STR) + Danger Sense (DEX), Bard Countercharm, Artificer Soul of Artifice, Sorcerer Tides of Chaos / Psychic Defenses, Cleric Corona of Light, Fighter/Cavalier Elegant Courtier, Champion Survivor (+3 death saves). **Racial traits are authored** via `RACE_SAVE_TRAITS` (matched by name against `character_data.race_traits`, text from `RACE_TRAIT_DESCRIPTIONS`, source label `Racial`, listed after class features): Brave, Lucky, Stout Resilience, Dwarven Resilience, Fey Ancestry, Gnome Cunning — all situational, so none tags a save row. **Scope rule:** only your OWN saves; a feature that gives an ENEMY disadvantage on ITS save (Eldritch Strike) stays on the surface where it fires.
